The story behind Sapling

One birthday. A mountain of toys. A simple question.

Could a present still feel personal if it was made for the child’s future, not just the day?

Why we started

It began with another well-meant present.

Like lots of friends, relatives and godparents, we wanted to give a child something useful. Something that might still matter years later.

At a birthday, we arrived carrying another present and saw it join an already enormous pile of toys. There was plenty of love in the room and plenty of thoughtful gifts. But ours felt like it might be enjoyed for a moment, then quietly disappear into the pile.

We were not against presents. Some toys become firm favourites, and birthdays should feel joyful. We simply wondered whether there should be another option too.

We would happily have contributed towards the child’s future. The awkward part was how. Asking the parents for bank details felt intrusive. Sending cash felt impersonal. The willingness to help was already there, but the simple, trusted way to invite it was not.

There should simply be a link.That small thought became the starting point for Sapling.

What we are building

A more natural way for a family’s goodwill to add up.

Sapling gives parents one private gift link to share with the people who care about their child. It makes one-off giving feel personal and simple, while keeping the important money stages clear.

For parents

One calm place to start

Create a private gift link for a child and share it when somebody asks what to buy.

For their people

A gift that still feels personal

Friends, relatives and godparents can make a one-off gift and leave a message without opening their own account.

For the future

More time for small gifts

Ordinary birthday and Christmas gifts can become part of a financial head start, with the investment provider responsible for holding and investing the money.

What matters to us

Useful, warm and honest about the money.

We are building Sapling around families, not finance jargon. That also means being clear about what has been paid, what is still being processed and what a provider has actually confirmed.

  • Joy now and possibility laterSapling is not here to replace every present. It gives families another thoughtful option.
  • Ordinary gifts are enough to beginA useful head start can be built by many people over time. It does not need one wealthy parent or one grand gesture.
  • More choices, not grand promisesMoney saved early might later help with education, housing, work, starting something or simply having a cushion. None of those outcomes is guaranteed.

Where we are now

Building carefully, learning with real families.

Sapling is currently in a small UK private beta. We are testing the parent and gift journey carefully, improving the parts that feel awkward and making sure the product never suggests that money has reached an investment provider before it has.

We are building it from the perspective that sparked the idea in the first place: friends, relatives and godparents who want to help, and parents who deserve an easier way to let them.
