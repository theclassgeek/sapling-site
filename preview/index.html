The advantage money can’t buy later

Starting sooner can matter more than giving more.

In this example, both families give exactly £11,050 in total. The only difference is when those gifts begin. Starting at birth gives the money five more years to grow—and creates roughly £4,400 more by 18.

“Start young… the nature of compound interest is it behaves like a snowball of sticky snow.”Warren Buffett, widely regarded as one of the most successful investors in modern history · Berkshire Hathaway annual meeting, 1999

Simple, transparent pricing

A small fee for making family generosity easier.

Sapling’s planned annual fee is 0.5% of the value in your child’s pot. That’s £5 a year for every £1,000. It helps us provide one simple, private place to collect gifts, follow what has arrived and stay connected with the right savings provider.
