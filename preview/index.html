Good to know

Clear answers before anyone gives.

Does all of my gift reach the investment account?

During the private beta, Sapling shows what you will be charged and separately tracks payment, allocation and provider confirmation. The final treatment of payment-processing costs is still being tested, so we will not describe a gift as invested until the provider confirms it. Any deduction that affects the amount sent to the provider must be shown clearly in the payment journey and receipt.

Who owns and controls the money?

Money held in a Junior ISA belongs to the child. A parent or guardian manages it while they are young; the child can take control at 16 and normally cannot withdraw it until 18.

What happens when my child turns 18?

The Junior ISA automatically becomes an adult ISA and your child gets access to it themselves. It includes all the money contributed, plus any interest or investment growth, after charges and any market losses. They can withdraw some or all of it, leave it invested in the adult ISA, or transfer that ISA to another provider. That means it can potentially keep growing until they need it for a home, another big purchase or a rainy day. Investments can fall as well as rise.

Is the 5% growth rate promised?

No. It is a single illustrative assumption used to explain compounding. It is not a provider forecast or guarantee, and actual investments can fall as well as rise.
