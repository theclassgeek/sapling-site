Privacy, in plain English

Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective 9 September 2026

Children’s and family information deserves extra care.

We collect only what we need to run and protect Sapling’s private beta, process gifts and coordinate the next operational step. We do not sell personal information, and partners do not receive child identities or household account values.

1. Who is responsible for your information?

Sapling is the controller of personal information used to provide the Sapling website and private-beta experience. During the beta, the individual or entity operating Sapling is identified in your invitation or onboarding communications. For privacy questions or requests, email hello@usesapling.co.uk.

Payment providers, Junior ISA providers and other financial-service providers may act as separate controllers for information they collect under their own terms and privacy notices.

2. Information we collect

  • Website and device information: IP address, browser and device details, requested pages, timestamps, security logs and basic hosting diagnostics.
  • Parent or guardian information: name, email, sign-in identity, contact details, beta answers and information needed to establish authority or coordinate a provider account.
  • Child information: name, date or expected date of birth, relationship to the parent, gift-page presentation and relevant Junior ISA or Child Trust Fund status.
  • Donor and gift information: donor name, email where requested, message, chosen amount, occasion, payment reference, method label and payment, refund or dispute status. Sapling does not receive full card or wallet credentials.
  • Account and operational information: fund, provider, transfer, allocation and confirmation status; contribution records; support history; and versioned authority or acceptance evidence.
  • Partner and referral information: partner contact and organisation details, application status, referral code or source, aggregate journey stages and campaign activity. Partners do not receive child names, messages or child-level financial values.
  • Communications: emails, support requests, feedback and records of choices or consents.

3. Where information comes from

Most information comes directly from parents, donors and partners. We may also receive payment status from Stripe, sign-in and database events from our technology providers, operational information from a Junior ISA provider when connected, and referral-source information from a tracked link. A parent may provide a child’s information and a donor may provide a message intended for that child or family.

5. Who we share information with

We share information only where needed with service providers and professional advisers, including:

  • Supabase for authentication, database, storage and secure server functions;
  • Stripe for hosted card and eligible wallet checkout, payment events, refunds and disputes;
  • Vercel and domain or infrastructure providers for hosting, delivery, security and diagnostics;
  • Junior ISA, Child Trust Fund or investment providers when a real provider relationship and lawful data flow are in place;
  • professional advisers, regulators, courts or law enforcement where reasonably necessary or legally required; and
  • a buyer or successor if the Sapling business is reorganised or transferred, subject to appropriate confidentiality and data-protection steps.

We do not sell personal information. We do not give partners child identities, donor messages, household account values or child-level investment activity.

6. International transfers

Some technology providers may process information outside the UK. Where UK personal information is transferred internationally, we use an applicable adequacy regulation or contractual and organisational safeguards designed to protect it. Contact us if you would like more information about the safeguard used for a particular provider.

7. How long we keep information

We keep information only for as long as needed for the purpose collected. Account and child-profile information is normally kept while the beta account is active and for a limited period afterwards so we can handle closure, provider coordination and complaints. Payment, authority, fraud, dispute and financial-operation records may need to be kept for up to six years, or longer where a legal claim or obligation requires it.

Unsuccessful applications, routine diagnostics and optional marketing details are kept for shorter periods where possible. When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it. We may retain a minimal suppression record so we can respect an opt-out.

8. Security

We use access controls, row-level database policies, encrypted connections, server-side payment verification, signed payment events and restricted administrative access. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Please use a strong, unique sign-in method and tell us promptly if you think an account or gift link has been misused.

9. Children’s information

A parent or guardian provides child information so Sapling can present the intended recipient, organise gifts and coordinate relevant provider steps. We do not ask children to create their own account in the current beta. Gift links reveal only the limited presentation information needed for the giving journey and should be shared carefully.

Money held in a Junior ISA belongs to the child. Data and account-control arrangements may need to change as the child approaches 16 or 18, and we will explain those changes before they take effect.

10. Cookies and local storage

The marketing site does not currently use advertising cookies. The application may use essential browser storage for sign-in, security and session continuity. Stripe may use essential technologies on its hosted checkout to process payments and prevent fraud. Our hosting and font providers receive ordinary technical request information when their resources are loaded.

If we introduce non-essential analytics or advertising technologies, we will update this policy and provide a suitable choice before using them where consent is required.

11. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data-protection law may give you the right to:

  • ask for a copy of your personal information;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • ask us to delete or restrict use of information;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests or to direct marketing;
  • receive certain information in a portable format; and
  • withdraw consent where consent is the basis we rely on.

These rights are not absolute. For example, we may need to keep payment or fraud records. Email hello@usesapling.co.uk to make a request. We may need to confirm your identity and your authority in relation to a child before acting.

12. Complaints

Please contact us first so we can investigate. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or by calling 0303 123 1113.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the beta, provider model or law changes. We will update the version date and explain material changes to account holders through the service or by email where appropriate.

14. Contact

Email hello@usesapling.co.uk for privacy questions, rights requests or concerns about a child’s information.
