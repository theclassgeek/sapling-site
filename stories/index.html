Our publishing promise

Proof before polish.

A story should make Sapling easier to understand. It should never overstate what happened or turn a planned feature into a customer result.

  • Real people, clear permissionIdentifiable founders, families, children, partners and experts appear only with recorded consent.
  • Real outcomes, carefully describedGift totals, payment status and provider-confirmed value stay separate. We do not invent success stories.
  • Useful context, not pressureEvery story should help somebody decide, without promising investment returns or suggesting one right way to give.
